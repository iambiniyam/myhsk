MyHSK human Mandarin word recordings

MyHSK mirrors matching 64 kbps recordings without changing their audio content
and maps them to the current HSK syllabus. The original project combines
recordings and curation from the Shtooka/SWAC Mandarin collection. See the
source repository for its complete attribution and history.

MyHSK source code is MIT-licensed. These audio recordings remain available
under their source license and are not relicensed under MIT.
