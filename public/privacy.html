Privacy, in plain language.

Effective August 2, 2026

MyHSK works without an account. Most learning data is stored in your browser unless you choose to create an account and synchronize it.

When you learn as a guest

Your vocabulary status, character mastery, preferences, and learning history stay on your device. You can export or delete this data through your browser and MyHSK settings.

When you create an account

We store your name, email address, sign-in provider, secure session information, and synchronized learning progress. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. We use this information to authenticate you and keep progress consistent across your devices.

Optional analytics

If enabled in settings, anonymous analytics count product events using random visitor and session identifiers. They do not include searched words, study content, email addresses, names, or account identifiers. You can turn analytics off at any time.

Service providers

Cloudflare hosts the app, authentication database, and synchronized progress. Better Auth provides the authentication software. When configured, Google handles Google sign-in and Resend delivers verification and password-reset emails. Each provider processes only the information needed for that service.

Your choices

  • Use all learning tools without an account.
  • Export or import local progress.
  • End individual account sessions.
  • Delete your account and synchronized progress from the account panel.
  • Disable anonymous analytics in settings.

Retention and security

Account data is retained while your account exists. Deleting an account removes its authentication records and synchronized progress. We use encrypted HTTPS connections, HTTP-only session cookies, email verification, access controls, and rate limits.

Updates

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above will be updated. Continued use is always optional, and guest learning remains available.
