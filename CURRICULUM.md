# MyHSK curriculum design

## Principle

The interface should stay simple while the curriculum remains deep.

The app therefore hides most pedagogy inside a single guided pack rather than presenting vocabulary, characters, grammar, reading, writing, and listening as unrelated products.

## Core learning unit

The learning unit is:

> character → word → collocation → sentence → retrieval → production

## Network types

### Word webs

A productive character anchors many compounds, such as:

> 开 → 开始、打开、开会、开发、开放、离开

### Sound families

Characters sharing a useful phonetic component are compared after the whole forms are shown.

### Meaning families

Semantic components organize characters without assuming that every radical is perfectly transparent.

### Scenario packs

Vocabulary is connected through practical situations such as meetings, debugging, travel, health, social media, reading, and daily life.

### Contrast labs

Near-synonyms and visually confusable forms are compared after separate contextual exposure.

## Pack structure

1. Overview the network.
2. Learn 3–5 anchors deeply.
3. Scan the larger connected vocabulary set.
4. Retrieve each item.
5. Read a sentence chain.
6. Produce one original sentence.
7. Review weak skills independently.

## Unlimited study

A pack is a cognitive chunk, not a quota. Learners can continue through as many packs as they want. The app recommends review when memories become fragile but never locks new content.
